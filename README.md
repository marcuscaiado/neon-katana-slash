## ✨ Features & Highlights

- 🍉 **Juicy Neon Fruit Arsenal**:
  - 🍉 **Watermelon** (50 pts) — Big, green rind with bright pink splash!
  - 🍊 **Orange** (75 pts) — Citrus burst with golden particle sparks.
  - 🍓 **Plasma Berry** (100 pts) — High-speed berry that requires fast blade reflex.
  - 🍍 **Pineapple** (150 pts) — Spiky tropical delicacy with bonus score.
  - ⭐ **Cyber Star** (200 pts) — Ultra-rare golden star fruit!
- ❄️ **Special Power-Up Fruits**:
  - ❄️ **Freeze Clock**: Slows down the entire arena into matrix bullet-time for effortless multi-fruit combos.
  - ⏱️ **Time Booster**: Adds valuable extra seconds to your match timer!
- 💣 **Glitch Bombs**: Avoid slicing glowing black/red bombs or suffer a severe score penalty and combo reset!
- ⚡ **Dynamic Combo System**: Slice 3 or more fruits in a single swift blade slash to earn multi-kill bonuses (`COMBO x3`, `COMBO x5`!).
- 🗡️ **Luminous Katana Trails**: Smooth, fading neon blade paths that track your finger or mouse swipe across the screen.
- 🔊 **Procedural Katana Audio**: Realistic sword whooshes, squishy fruit slices, and explosion booms via the Web Audio API.
- 🏆 **Global Arcade Leaderboard**: Integrated with Marcus Arcade to persist high scores and world records.
